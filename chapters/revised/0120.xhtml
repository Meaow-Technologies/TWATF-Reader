Ethan’s eyes, or rather, the eyes of Rachel Young, whom Ethan had possessed, shook violently.

The Dolls clinging to Jaehwan were not real. They were all illusions created by Ethan’s Sanctuary.

Still, even if they were illusions, destroying something created by a Sanctuary was not easy.

What in the world was this Sanctuary?

Could a world like this even be called a Sanctuary?

“This is not Andersen’s Sanctuary. How did you achieve Sanctuary Manifestation?”

A Sanctuary was power only a god could use.

Yet the human standing before Ethan was not Andersen’s Vicegerent. Nor did Jaehwan seem to be another god’s Vicegerent.

“Does that matter?”

Ethan’s gaze changed at Jaehwan’s indifferent reply.

“It does. If you are the world Andersen chose.”

A Sanctuary was the current world as seen by a god.

In other words, every Sanctuary was influenced by the history and imagination of the god who had lived within it.

With every step Jaehwan took, the world around his footprints changed. Blood-soaked pools multiplied, and graveyards of lion bones rose from within them. The thick fog drifting from the pools obscured the distant view of the Worldview, but if this was what its entrance looked like, there was no need to see any further.

“What a childish imagination, Andersen. Is this all you chose…”

The next moment, Ethan saw a strange sight.

Beyond the fog of Jaehwan’s Unique World, there was something bizarre.

Just as Ethan was about to speak of it, Jaehwan said, “Your Worldview does not look particularly special either.”

Dokbul tore through Ethan’s World Force and surged forward in an instant.

Rachel Young’s fragile neck, inhabited by Ethan, was about to be severed.

“I surrender.”

“…What?”

“I surrender.”

Ethan suddenly raised both hands.

Jaehwan tied Ethan up with Runald.

“Jaehwan-nim, will this be enough to restrain them?”

“If you want to restrain me, purchase this.”

Ethan twisted about a little, testing the restraints and chair’s durability. Soon, a satisfied smile appeared on their face.

“Excellent. As expected of the Parts I made.”

Could Jaehwan not just kill this bastard?

[Endure it. They said they will listen to us, after all. No matter how they look, Ethan is one of the Metaverse Six Gods. Do not underestimate them.]

Ethan’s intentions were impossible to read. What was with the sudden surrender? And why did Ethan ask to be restrained, only to sell them their own Parts? No god in their right mind would come up with something like that.

[They have always been impossible to understand. They were the same five hundred years ago.]

Even so, Andersen’s voice held a subtle warmth.

“Excuse me, Jaehwan-ssi.”

“Are you planning to kill Ethan-nim?”

The villagers had approached Jaehwan without him noticing. Their eyes were too pure to be fake. It was strange. They were Andersen’s former Followers, so why were they defending Ethan?

“Ethan turned you into Dolls. Why are you worried about them?”

“Because Ethan-nim turned us into Dolls.”

It was a strange answer.

“We asked Ethan-nim to do it.”

Hans added quietly, a sorrowful smile on his face.

“We asked Ethan-nim to turn us into Dolls.”

* * *

[They probably did it to keep me alive.]

Five hundred years ago, Andersen had been driven out and forcibly sealed.

[As you know, a god who remains forgotten for too long in the 《Abyss》 disappears.]

The foundation of a god was a concept.

Naturally, concepts forgotten by many people disappeared.

No one knew whether the Dolls’ prayers had worked. Still, Andersen had not disappeared even after the seal was lifted.

Then she finally returned to this Tower.

[The Followers were all afraid of becoming that bastard’s Dolls, but Ethan has never ignored someone’s will and forced Dollification on them.]

Maybe Ethan was not as bad as Jaehwan had thought.

[I said Ethan was crazy. I never said they were bad. To begin with, Ethan only asks Followers on the verge of death whether they want to become Dolls.]

It still made no sense. If that was the case, why had Ethan not explained it from the beginning?

You seemed to be charging in to kill Ethan earlier too.

[They crossed a line when they touched my former Vicegerent.]

What Jaehwan did not understand was why Ethan had not explained that from the beginning.

“Are you giving us a chance to ask now?”

When Jaehwan turned around, Ethan was looking at him with a shrug.

“Well, I am completely restrained now. Ask whatever you want.”

With Ethan acting like that, Jaehwan could not even remember what he had wanted to ask.

When Jaehwan remained silent, Ethan began rambling first.

“You asked about the Dolls earlier. First of all, the Dolls I made…”

“My friend was captured here. I heard they were taken away to become a Doll.”

Ethan abruptly fell silent, then answered with a sharp glare.

“That is common enough. So?”

“I want to find my friend.”

Ethan’s expression grew complicated.

“It is a touching story, but I do not think I have any reason to help you. I do not purchase souls to make Dolls.”

“I heard you are the first Creator of Dolls. You must have connections with the other Creators in the Tower.”

“Well, it is not as if there is no way to look into it. But.”

Ethan studied Jaehwan in silence before speaking.

“Why should I help you?”

“Because you will die if you do not.”

Jaehwan gripped Dokbul’s hilt ominously. Yet Ethan did not seem disturbed in the slightest.

“If you kill me, this Doll will die too. Would your ‘Companion,’ Andersen, truly want that?”

[That fucking bastard, again.]

Just as the enraged Andersen was about to snap back, a Follower screamed.

“Fire!”

Smoke rose from where the Follower pointed.

A moment later, horrified cries came from the villagers.

“Oh my god.”

The fire had broken out in the village warehouse. The villagers rushed over and extinguished it, revealing the desolate interior.

Something was wrong.

“It is gone!”

The Follower of the low-grade god, who had been locked in the warehouse just yesterday, had vanished.

More precisely, they seemed to be dead.

Their soul form had been burned black. It was divine punishment.

Andersen muttered.

[Goddammit, this is…]

Among the traces of the burned soul form was a red brand.

Andersen immediately recognized it.

The Alliance’s Brand.

It was a brand she had seen five hundred years ago as well.

Every village that received that brand had disappeared without a trace.

The Alliance had made its declaration. It would hold this village responsible for the death.

Ethan, who had wheeled themself over in the restraint chair, muttered, “What the hell did you people do in my village?”
