Black Moon Inn.

Galateon's cold killing intent raining down on them. The overwhelming certainty that he would die if he fought.

Was it really right to run away like this?

One of the Alliance members who had been chasing them stood there.

Andersen recognized them and stepped forward.

"Jihan? It is you, isn't it?"

At least, once upon a time.

Jihan smiled quietly.

"I am not the Jihan you knew. You should know well enough what I have done."

The God Without a Vicegerent, Ethan Cart's most cherished Incarnation.

And then...

The Vicegerent who had betrayed them five hundred years ago.

Jihan pointed the tip of their staff at them threateningly.

"Damn it. How could you... how could you..."

"You will die if you keep that. Hand over the cube, then leave."

Leave?

"Then I will spare you, Andersen, and that Follower of yours. I will also guarantee the King of Crows's safety."

"How can I trust that?"

"I swear it to the God of Whirlwinds... no, to the King of Crows."

Andersen glanced down at the cube in Runald's hand.

The cracks across its surface had deepened considerably.

The Moya Moya Crow Doll's spring creaked.

"Jihan. Is that truly the path you chose? Have you forgotten every oath you made five hundred years ago? Has your cause, the one where you wanted to make a world where gods and humans were equal, all disappeared?"

"Rats and cats cannot be friends."

"What...?"

"If a rat wants to befriend a cat, there is only one way. It has to become a rat as strong as a cat."

Runald looked up at Andersen.

Jihan's words resembled the story Andersen had once told him about a strong rat.

"I just fought a member of Rupture. After fighting them, I understood exactly how strong they are."

"Wait, you."

Jihan's eerily gleaming eyes were fixed on the cube.

"I need that cube."

"What I want is not the King of Crows. Andersen, there is no point hiding it. I already know who he is."

Jihan smiled coldly.

"I will devour him and become an Awakened One."

There was no way for Jihan, who was already another god's Follower, to become an Awakened One.

Still, there might be a way to steal an Awakened One's power.

It was something Andersen herself had once attempted.

God Devouring.

"It seems you have forgotten who I am."

Jihan took a grimy black glove from their clothes. At a glance, it was clearly a dangerous Part.

Jihan had been Ethan's former Vicegerent. Perhaps they had inherited Ethan's techniques and created a Part that allowed a human to perform God Devouring.

Andersen growled and beat her wings.

"I can't let you do that."

"If you refuse to yield, then I will have to use force..."

"Jihan, what are you doing?"

Beris approached, gritting his teeth through a body reduced to rags.

"Do not interfere."

The Alliance's promised later arrivals had only been Mogi, led by Galateon. That was what Jihan had clearly heard.

Then what was that?

Only a handful of gods in the Metaverse possessed such status.

[Jihan, why did you steal the Alliance's cube?]

Currently ranked fourth in the Metaverse.

Leader of Alliance Bihwa.

The God of Assassination, Rakers.

Having served the Alliance for the past five hundred years, they knew.

No matter what they said here, they would die.

Then there was only one option left.

"Andersen! Hand over the cube now! Otherwise, we will all die!"

Including Galateon, who was engaged in fierce combat within the volcanic crater, two of the Metaverse Six Gods had arrived on this battlefield.

Behind them stood hundreds of Alliance members from Mogi and Bihwa.

Even if Jaehwan and Ethan returned, facing all of them at once would be impossible.

"Andersen-nim."

At last, it was time to choose.

In a wide clearing, Jaehwan stabbed into the air.

「World Stab」

He attacked again and again, but the cube's wall would not break easily. Instead, its surface produced a powerful rebound that frequently sent the force of his Stab back at him.

Jaehwan prepared a stronger attack.

"I told you it would be better to stop. If you strike with greater force, the recoil will injure us."

At Ethan's warning, Jaehwan stopped his Stab and glared at the wall.

「Doubt」

An entire day passed as Jaehwan repeatedly stabbed at the cube wall or stared at it. Yet even with his 「Doubt」, it was difficult to grasp its essence. He could only see the strange movements of particles, as though they were not matter from this world.

Ethan slowly examined the wall.

"There are traces of a manipulation-type Setting. It looks like an exceptional craftsman made this."

"A Dream Demon's work?"

"Probably. It seems this zone underwent Sanctuary Conversion using the Tears of the Fall of Time, but I cannot be sure."

Jaehwan stabbed at the wall once more and asked, "How do we get out?"

"Not right now. Even if I dismantle it my way, it will take at least ten days."

If they were trapped for ten days, not even fragments of Runald or Andersen's souls would remain. He would not be able to save Yoonhwan either.

There was no time to wait that long.

"Do not worry. Time flows differently here. Not even a minute has passed outside yet."

"Is that why you are lounging around and doing business?"

As Jaehwan said, Ethan had even set up a luxurious recliner. They lay back in it leisurely, checking the sales status of Products on the auction house. All the while, settled payments continued flowing into their pockets.

"It is a form of survival. Unlike you, I need to consume trust to maintain my existence. To eat, I need to earn."

"The other gods do not seem to be like that."

Andersen had consumed trust before, but that had been to urgently replenish her World Force.

Ethan answered.

"Gods with Followers do not need to go through that empty ritual. Their World Force gradually increases and recovers even if they do nothing. But I am different."

Jaehwan recalled Ethan's divine title.

God Without a Vicegerent.

Ethan did not merely lack a Vicegerent. They were a god without even a single Follower.

Ethan added, "A god with no Followers cannot recover or increase their World Force. It declines over time. Without replenishing it, they eventually disappear."

"So that is why you avoided fighting."

Jaehwan had wondered why Ethan suddenly surrendered in their recent battle. It turned out they had wanted to conserve their World Force.

"Your luck was good."

"I wonder. Yours was probably better."

"Why do you not use a Vicegerent?"

"That makes it sound rather romantic."

"You do not have to tell me."

"They were the one who stole my Doll-making methods, and they were also the first to cling to the Alliance and betray us."

Jaehwan's sword changed direction.

A tremendous gale from his Stab grazed Ethan's earlobe. Ethan's hair whipped violently, but their expression remained blank, as though they trusted Jaehwan not to attack them.

That irritated Jaehwan.

"Are all gods of the Abyss as talkative as you?"

"Some are like me, and some are not. Most Alliance members do not have Vicegerents either."

"..."

Worms.

Jaehwan's hand paused as he continued stabbing.

Ethan went on.

"They want to become complete gods on their own, without the restrictions of Followers or Vicegerents. That is why they create constructs like Dolls and use them as Vicegerents. The worms they ride do not need wills of their own."

Gods who looked down on humans.

He could not understand it.

Who would pray to an absolute being that viewed them as nothing but worms or expendable goods?

There were certainly gods like Andersen in the Abyss.

Low-grade gods who cared for their Followers, gods who could be called good in the ordinary sense.

「“I was a Follower of Andersen-nim.”」

「“This one is a Follower of Hermier...”」

Come to think of it, that was true.

Just as gods needed Followers, Followers needed gods.

The god they acted as Vicegerents for defined their worth.

The 《Abyss》 was a world where those who could not act as their own Vicegerents relied on one another to reveal their existence.

"Because you are someone who can act as their own Vicegerent."

The faces of enemies who had spat hatred at him came to mind.

Lords acted as Vicegerents for the System.

Gods could not exist without Vicegerents, and Followers needed gods to prove themselves.

Then what was Jaehwan?

Neither a Lord nor a god nor a Follower.

"Seeing your Sanctuary made me wonder. Are you truly acting as your own Vicegerent?"

What he acted as a Vicegerent for.

"Are you not also merely a Follower acting as a Vicegerent for your own Worldview?"

Samyeong Garam had once said that Adapters and Awakened Ones were separated by only a sheet of paper.

They simply lived while believing in different worlds.

Then, just as Samyeong Garam had been a slave to the System, perhaps Jaehwan too was no more than a slave to the world he believed in.

"I..."

Was his world entirely his?

"You will know when you see it yourself."

Sanctuary Manifestation.
