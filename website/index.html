✒ About the Author

Sing Shong (싱숑) is a married author duo who first met while studying creative writing at Dongguk University. Working in tandem, Sing leads the core plotting and worldbuilding while Shong refines the prose, character dialogue, and editing.

Beyond The World After the Fall (their debut work), they are renowned worldwide as the creators of Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint and How to Become a Star.

The duo maintains strict personal anonymity and communicates with their fans exclusively through author notes, postscript remarks, and occasional written Q&As.
